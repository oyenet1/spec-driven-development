# Alex Hormozi's "$100M Offers" Execution Skill

This skill implements the offer creation methodologies from Alex Hormozi's *$100M Offers*, enabling the creation of high-ticket, premium-margin offers that eliminate price resistance.

## Core Directives & Playbooks

1. **The Value Equation Optimization:**
   - $\text{Value} = \frac{\text{Dream Outcome} \times \text{Perceived Likelihood}}{\text{Time Delay} \times \text{Effort \& Sacrifice}}$
   - Focus on cutting time delay to near-zero and removing customer effort.
2. **The 5-Step Grand Slam Offer Blueprint:**
   - Step 1: Identify Starving Crowd (Massive Pain, Purchasing Power, Easy to Target, Growing).
   - Step 2: List all 20+ obstacles and problems.
   - Step 3: Convert problems into solution statements.
   - Step 4: Map delivery vehicles across the Delivery Cube (1-on-1, Small Group, 1-to-Many; DIY, DWY, DFY).
   - Step 5: Trim & Stack for high margin.
3. **Offer Enhancement Elements:**
   - Scarcity (Capacity & Cohort limits).
   - Urgency (Rolling deadlines & Price hike notices).
   - Bonus Stacking (Solving the next subsequent problem).
   - Risk Reversal Guarantees (Unconditional, Conditional, Anti-Guarantee, Implied/Performance).
   - The M.A.G.I.C. Naming Formula (Magnetic Reason, Announce Avatar, Give Goal, Indicate Interval, Complete with Container).
