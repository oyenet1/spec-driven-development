# Direct-Response Advertising & Landing Page Wireframe Blueprints

> **Core Philosophy:** "An ad does not exist to win design awards; it exists to buy customers at a profit. A landing page has only one goal: to guide the reader through an emotional, logical, and risk-reversed slippery slope straight into conversion." — Sabri Suby

---

## 1. High-Converting Meta/Facebook Ad Copy Architectures

Direct-response ads on Meta (Facebook/Instagram), TikTok, and LinkedIn must capture attention, agitate the core problem, deliver an emotional story or insight, and issue a direct call to action.

### Structure 1: The Power 4 Meta Ad Blueprint (Sabri Suby Method)

```text
[LINE 1 - THE PATTERN INTERRUPT HOOK]
ATTENTION [Target Avatar]: If you are tired of [Biggest Frustration], stop scrolling right now.

[LINES 2-4 - THE BLEEDING NECK AGITATION]
Most [Avatars] make the fatal mistake of [Common Ineffective Tactic].
They burn through thousands on [Tools/Agencies/Methods] and end up with [Nightmare Scenario].
I know because I did the exact same thing for [Timeframe]...

[LINES 5-8 - THE EPIPHANY STORY]
Until I discovered a counter-intuitive secret used by the top 1% of [Industry]:
Instead of [Old Hard Way], we flipped the process and deployed [Unique Mechanism].

[LINES 9-12 - PROOF & RESULTS]
The results?
✓ [Specific Metric 1: e.g., Revenue climbed by 247%]
✓ [Specific Metric 2: e.g., Cost per acquisition dropped by 64%]
✓ [Specific Metric 3: e.g., Scaled to $100k/mo without working weekends]

[LINES 13-16 - THE HVCO INTRODUCTION]
I've condensed this exact step-by-step strategy into a free, actionable 15-page guide called:
"The [HVCO Title]"

[LINE 17 - DIRECT COMMAND CTA]
Click the link below right now to download your free copy before this promo ends:

👉 [LINK URL]
```
