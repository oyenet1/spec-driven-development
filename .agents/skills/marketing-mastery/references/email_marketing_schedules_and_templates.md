| Campaign Type | Recommended Send Days | Recommended Send Times (Recipient's Timezone) | Strategic Reason |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| **Weekly Value / Newsletter** | Tuesday or Thursday | **10:00 AM** or **2:00 PM** | Peak working concentration, high desktop/mobile readership. |

| **Cold Lead Re-engagement** | Wednesday or Sunday evening | **8:30 PM** | Casual, non-intrusive personal check-in. |

## 2. Formatting & Deliverability Rules (Getting Into the Primary Inbox)

1. **Strict Plain-Text / Minimalist HTML:**
   - Avoid heavy image banners, multiple columns, and HTML templates.
   - Plain-text emails look personal and bypass Google's "Promotions" and "Spam" tabs straight to the "Primary" inbox.
2. **Logo Placement:**
   - **Never put your logo at the top** (screams marketing promo). Put your logo in the signature footer only.
3. **The Slippery Slope Copywriting Method:**
   - Keep sentences short (1–2 sentences per line).
   - Use double line breaks to make scanning frictionless.
4. **Subject Line Rules:**
   - **Pattern Interrupt Subject Lines:** Use either ultra-short (2–4 words, all lowercase or sentence case: `quick question`, `did you see this?`, `bad news...`) OR ultra-long, hyper-specific headlines.
   - Avoid generic title-cased corporate text (e.g., *"KingKong Monthly Newsletter Volume 4"*).
5. **Clear Single Call to Action:**
   - Have 1 primary goal per email with 2–3 links to the same destination (top, middle, and P.S.).
