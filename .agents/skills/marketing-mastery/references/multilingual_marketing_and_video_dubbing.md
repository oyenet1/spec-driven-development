## 3. Cultural Persuasion & Adaptation Rules

When writing in another language, you must adapt to local cultural buying drivers rather than doing literal machine translation:

### 1. Latin America (Spanish & Portuguese)
- **Buying Drivers:** Passion, family advancement, community, warmth, personal freedom.
- **Copy Tone:** High-energy, warm, empathetic, enthusiastic.
- **Key Channels:** WhatsApp Business, Instagram DMs, Facebook groups.
- **Formatting:** Frequent emojis, exclamation points, bold friendly guarantees.

### 2. Francophone West Africa (French & Local Slang)
- **Buying Drivers:** Practical utility, trust in origin, family protection, affordability.
- **Payment Realities:** Mobile Money (Wave, Orange Money, MTN MoMo) instead of credit cards.
- **Key Channels:** WhatsApp groups, TikTok, Facebook.

### 3. Germany & Northern Europe (German, Dutch)
- **Buying Drivers:** Efficiency, precision, risk reduction, certifications, data.
- **Copy Tone:** Professional, authoritative, zero hype, detailed specifications.
- **Formatting:** Emphasize DIN standards, ISO certifications, exact technical steps, and rock-solid written warranties.

### 4. Nigeria & West Africa (Nigerian Pidgin, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo)
- **Buying Drivers:** Immediate proof, eliminating the fear of scams, prestige/status, fast cash flow, sachet affordability.
- **Tone:** Street-smart, relatable, respectful ("Chief", "Chairman", "Blessed morning"), proof-heavy.
- **Channels:** WhatsApp Status, DMs, phone calls before dispatch.

## 5. How to Prompt the Agent for Written Copy in Any Language

Simply specify the language in your prompt:

1. *"Write a 17-step sales page in **Spanish** for my real estate investment program."*
2. *"Create a 4-email welcome sequence in **French** for our B2B SaaS platform."*
3. *"Write a 3-part Facebook ad campaign in **Nigerian Pidgin** selling solar generators in Port Harcourt."*
4. *"Translate and culturally adapt our English landing page into **German**, ensuring the tone is data-backed and professional with strong warranty focus."*
5. *"Write a WhatsApp status selling sequence in **Portuguese** for personal trainers in Brazil."*
