# Sabri Suby's "Sell Like Crazy" Master Playbook

> **Core Philosophy:** "The only thing that matters in business is customer acquisition. If you cannot reliably and profitably acquire customers at scale, you do not have a business—you have a hobby that costs you money." — Sabri Suby

## 1. The Larger Market Formula

Most businesses fail because they compete only for the **3%** of the market who are ready to buy right now. The real fortune lies in capturing the **97%** before your competitors even know they exist.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  3%  BUYING NOW (High competition, hyper-expensive)    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 17%  INFORMATION GATHERING (Comparing options)         │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 20%  PROBLEM AWARE (Know they have pain, no solution)  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 60%  NOT PROBLEM AWARE (Completely unconscious of pain) │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The Strategic Shift:
- **Traditional Marketing:** Yells "Buy my product!" to the 3%. Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) skyrockets.
- **The Sell Like Crazy System:** Uses a **High-Value Content Offer (HVCO)** to attract the 97% who are problem-aware or information-gathering, educates them via the **Magic Lantern Technique**, and converts them into buyers with a **Godfather Offer**.

## 2. Phase 1: Understand & Identify Your Dream Buyer (The Halo Strategy)

Before writing a single word of copy, you must get inside the mind of your prospect. You must know them better than their spouse or therapist.

### The 9 Dream Buyer Avatar Questions:
1. **Where do they spend time online?** (Specific Facebook groups, subreddits, forums, YouTube channels, podcasts)
2. **Where do they spend time offline?** (Conferences, sporting events, clubs, neighborhoods)
3. **What are their biggest frustrations and pain points?** (What keeps them tossing and turning at 2:00 AM?)
4. **What are their deepest fears?** (Financial ruin, public embarrassment, failure, disease, losing status)
5. **What are their primary goals and desires?** (Freedom, recognition, wealth, health, attraction)
6. **What is their preferred form of communication?** (WhatsApp, email, phone calls, short-form video)
7. **What language and slang do they use?** (Exact phrases they use when venting in forums)
8. **What looks like a typical day in their life?** (Hour-by-hour emotional and physical journey)
9. **What makes them happy and gives them hope?** (The ideal "after" state)

### Field Research Process:
- **Reddit & Quora Search:** Search keywords + "how do I", "hate when", "struggling with". Note exact phrases.
- **Amazon 1-Star & 5-Star Reviews:** Read 1-star reviews of competitor books/products to find unmet desires and deep frustrations. Read 5-star reviews to find exact transformation language.
- **Competitor Ad Scrape:** Use Meta Ad Library to observe competitor ads running for >90 days (if running long, they are profitable).

## 3. Phase 2: Create The Perfect Bait (High-Value Content Offer - HVCO)

The HVCO is an irresistible piece of free content that solves a specific fraction of your dream buyer's problem and positions your paid solution as the natural next step.

### The 8 Rules for a Killer HVCO:
1. **Solves a specific problem:** Don't give a generic 200-page encyclopedia. Give a sharp scalpel.
2. **Promises a quick win:** The reader must be able to consume it in 10–20 minutes and achieve a result within 24–48 hours.
3. **High perceived value:** Must look and feel like something you could easily charge $97–$297 for.
4. **Instant gratification:** Delivered digitally within 3 seconds of opt-in.
5. **Specific & tangible title:** Uses numbers, timeframes, and eliminates pain.
6. **Bridges to your core offer:** Solves Problem A, which naturally uncovers Problem B (your paid product/service).
7. **Easy to consume:** Checklists, cheat sheets, blueprints, templates, scorecards, swipe files, or short teardown videos.
8. **Never pitch product in the title:** The title must be 100% educational and value-driven.

### High-Converting HVCO Title Formulas:
- `The [Number]-Step Blueprint to [Desirable Outcome] in [Timeframe] (Without [Biggest Frustration])`
  *Example:* "The 7-Step Blueprint to Booking 15 High-Ticket B2B Sales Calls in 14 Days (Without Cold Calling or Expensive Ads)"
- `The [Adjective] Cheatsheet: How to [Desired Goal] Even If [Biggest Obstacle]`
  *Example:* "The Anti-Burnout Cheatsheet: How to Double Your Agency Revenue Even If You Work Solo"
- `[Number] Costly Mistakes [Target Audience] Make When [Action] (And What to Do Instead)`
  *Example:* "9 Costly Mistakes Nigerian E-commerce Founders Make with Facebook Ads (And the 3-Step Fix)"

## 4. Phase 3: Lead Capture & Landing Page Architecture

The landing page has one job: **Capture contact details in exchange for the HVCO.**

### Wireframe Anatomy of a 20%+ Converting Opt-In Page:
1. **Eyebrow/Preheader:** `[Free Download / Instant Access / For [Avatar] Only]`
2. **Attention-Grabbing Headline:** Clear, bold, curiosity-driven big promise.
3. **Subheadline:** Validates the headline, explains what is inside, and removes skepticism.
4. **Visual Product Mockup:** 3D book cover, cheat sheet graphic, tablet/mobile screen showing the asset.
5. **Fascinating Bullet Points (6–8 bullets):** Each bullet highlights a specific secret, counter-intuitive insight, or pain-killer found inside. Use "The Little-Known Secret to...", "How to...", "Why [Common Myth] is killing your results".
6. **Short Opt-in Form:** First Name + Best Email + Phone/WhatsApp (if SMS/WhatsApp follow-up is used).
7. **High-Urgency CTA Button:** First-person action language: `"Send Me My Free Blueprint Now >>"`
8. **Micro-Copy Trust Badge:** "100% Free. No Spam. Your Information Is Secure."

## 5. Phase 4: The Godfather Offer Strategy & The 17-Step Secret Selling System

> "Make them an offer so good they feel stupid saying no, and an offer so compelling that only a lunatic would refuse." — Sabri Suby

### The 7 Components of a Godfather Offer:
1. **The Giant Promise:** Bold, measurable, clear transformation.
2. **Unmatched Value Stack:** Show that the total value of items received is at least 10x the asking price.
3. **Compelling Price Framing:** Price anchor against expensive alternatives or the cost of inaction.
4. **Irresistible Bonuses:** Valuable standalone assets that eliminate friction and speed up results.
5. **Ironclad Risk Reversal Guarantee:** Shift 100% of the risk from the buyer to you (e.g., "300% Money-Back Guarantee", "Pay on Delivery", "If you don't hit X, we work for free until you do").
6. **Genuine Scarcity & Urgency:** Hard deadlines, limited spots, or bonus expiries that force decision-making.
7. **Crystal Clear CTA:** One single, unambiguous next step.

## 6. Phase 5: Paid Traffic & The Power 4 Meta Ad Framework

Traffic is not a mystery; it is simple math. If you can pay $50 to acquire a customer worth $200, you have an infinite money printing machine.

### Key Unit Economics Metrics:
- **Cost Per Lead (CPL):** Total Ad Spend / Leads Generated
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA):** Total Ad Spend / Customers Acquired
- **Lifetime Value (LTV):** Net profit generated per customer over their lifespan
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS):** Revenue / Ad Spend

### The Power 4 Meta Ad Framework:
Every high-converting direct response Facebook/Instagram ad contains four distinct components:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 1. THE HOOK / HEADLINE                                  │
│    - Pattern interrupt image/video thumbnail            │
│    - Calling out the avatar + provocative question      │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 2. THE LEAD                                             │
│    - Gripping first 2 lines (before "See More" button)  │
│    - Story hook, shocking statistic, or relatable pain  │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 3. THE BODY COPY (Story + Agitation + Solution)        │
│    - Visceral storytelling of the struggle              │
│    - Why traditional methods fail                       │
│    - Introduce the HVCO as the breakthrough discovery    │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 4. THE CALL TO ACTION (CTA)                             │
│    - Specific command: "Click the link below to get..." │
│    - Low-friction prompt + URL link in copy             │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### The 3 Lengths of Ad Copy to Test:
1. **Short Copy (50–100 words):** Quick punchy hook, 3 bullet points, direct CTA to HVCO.
2. **Medium Copy (150–300 words):** Problem -> Agitation -> Discovery -> HVCO Solution.
3. **Long Copy / Story Ad (500–1,200 words):** Full narrative journey, emotional hook, villain identification, breakthrough epiphany, value breakdown, CTA. (Sabri's testing shows long copy consistently produces higher quality leads).

## 7. Phase 6: The Magic Lantern Technique & Email Conversion Machine

The Magic Lantern Technique guides undecided leads (the 97%) down a path of goodwill by giving them value in advance, step by step, showing them how to get closer to their dream outcome.

### The Magic Lantern Sequence:
- **Video 1 (Day 1):** The Big Domino / Mindset Shift. Reveal why their previous attempts failed and introduce the new strategy.
- **Video 2 (Day 3):** The Core Framework / Strategy Teardown. Walk through the exact step-by-step framework to execute.
- **Video 3 (Day 5):** The Implementation Blueprint & Godfather Offer. Show real-world case studies and pitch the Strategy Session / Sales Call.

---

### Email Marketing Cadence & Sending Rules:

#### 1. Optimal Send Timing:
- **Best Day:** **Tuesday** (highest global open and click-through rates across industries).
- **Secondary Best Days:** **Thursday** (strong mid-week engagement) and **Sunday Evening** (people planning their week ahead).
- **Best Time:** **10:00 AM local time** (post-morning inbox cleanup) or **8:00 PM local time** (leisure mobile browsing).
- **Avoid:** Monday morning (inbox overwhelm) and Friday afternoon (weekend checkout).

#### 2. Design Rules:
- **Plain Text / Minimal HTML Only:** Looks like a personal email from a friend. No heavy corporate headers, banners, or multi-column layouts that trigger the "Promotions" or "Spam" tab.
- **Logo in the Email Signature Only**, never at the top.
- **Short Paragraphs:** 1–2 sentences per line. Create a "slippery slope" where the eye effortlessly slides down the page.
- **Subject Lines:** Either ultra-short (2–4 words, all lowercase or sentence case: e.g., `quick question`, `bad news...`, `did you see this?`) or super long and hyper-specific. Avoid title-cased corporate headlines.
- **Preheader Text:** Must burn with curiosity, completing the subject line.

## 8. Phase 7: Sales Conversion — "Sell Like a Doctor"

> **Golden Rule:** "A prescription without a diagnosis is malpractice. Never pitch your product until you have thoroughly diagnosed their pain, their previous failures, and their dream outcome."

### The 2-Step Consultative Closing Process:

#### Step 1: The 15-Minute Triage Call (Lead Qualification)
- **Purpose:** Determine fit, eliminate tire-kickers, and set the frame.
- **Key Questions:**
  1. "What made you reach out today?"
  2. "What are you currently doing to solve [problem]?"
  3. "What's working and what's broken?"
  4. "What is your target revenue / dream goal over the next 6–12 months?"
  5. "If we show you a customized plan to hit that goal, are you in a position to invest in executing it?"
- **Outcome:** If qualified, book the 45-minute Strategy Session. If not qualified, redirect to free resources.

#### Step 2: The 45-Minute Strategy Session Script (The Close)
1. **Set the Agenda & Take Control:** "The goal of this call is to review your current numbers, identify your biggest bottlenecks, and map out a customized roadmap. At the end, if it's a mutual fit, we can discuss working together. If not, you take the plan for free. Fair enough?"
2. **Deep Dive Diagnosis (Uncover Pain & Inaction Cost):** "How long has this been an issue? What is it costing you each month to NOT fix this?"
3. **Paint the Dream Future:** "If we wave a magic wand and 12 months from now you're at [Dream Goal], what does your life look like?"
4. **Present the Customized Prescription:** Connect each of their specific pains directly to your solution's modules.
5. **State the Investment with Authority:** State the price without flinching or stammering. Then **shut up and listen**. (The first person to speak loses).
6. **Handle Objections with Empathy and Reframing:** Isolate the real objection (Finances, Partner, Time, or Fear of Failure) and guide them to a decision.
