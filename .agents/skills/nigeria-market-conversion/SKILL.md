# Nigerian Market Conversion & WhatsApp Commerce Skill

This skill implements the marketing principles of Akin Alabi (*How to Sell to Nigerians*, *Small Business Big Money*) and Mofe Richard (*Sell Like Crazy on WhatsApp*), tailored specifically for Nigerian consumer behavior and digital ecosystems.

## Core Directives & Playbooks

1. **Overcoming the Acute Trust Deficit:**
   - Overwhelming proof stacking: Video unboxings, physical store address in Lagos/Abuja, CAC registration, and customer bank alerts.
2. **Emotional Hot-Button Copy:**
   - Write to Nigerian core emotional drivers: Fear of loss/embarrassment/poverty, Greed/quick wealth, Status/Owambe prestige, Japa/travel, Beauty/vitality, and Marriage.
3. **Payment on Delivery (POD) Optimization:**
   - Execute the 4-step dispatch confirmation protocol (Instant WhatsApp message -> Phone call -> Morning reminder -> Pre-payment greed discount) to keep return rates under 8%.
4. **WhatsApp Conversational Sales Engine:**
   - 4-Part Daily WhatsApp Status Flow (Lifestyle -> Educational Tip -> Bank Alert Proof -> Limited Stock CTA).
   - 1-on-1 DM diagnostic and closing script with audio voice note strategy.
5. **Akin Alabi's Direct Response Sales Letter:**
   - Big Bold Headline with "Even If" factor, Fear/Embarrassment hook, "FREE" bonus stacking (Free delivery, free gift), Ironclad guarantee, and WhatsApp checkout button.
