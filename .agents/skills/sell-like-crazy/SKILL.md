# Sabri Suby's "Sell Like Crazy" Execution Skill

This skill implements the exact 8-Phase customer acquisition system from Sabri Suby's *Sell Like Crazy*, responsible for over $1.33 billion in client sales across 416 industries.

## The 8-Phase Sales Machine

1. **Phase 1: Understand & Identify Dream Buyer (The Halo Strategy)**
   - Answer the 9 dream avatar questions.
   - Mine Reddit, Quora, and Amazon 1-star/5-star reviews for visceral language.
2. **Phase 2: Create the Perfect Bait (High-Value Content Offer - HVCO)**
   - High-perceived-value cheatsheet, blueprint, scorecard, or teardown guide.
   - 8-rule compliance: Quick win, highly specific, solves Problem A to bridge to Problem B.
3. **Phase 3: Lead Capture & Landing Pages**
   - High-converting 20%+ opt-in page with 3D product mockup, 6 curiosity bullets, and single-field forms.
4. **Phase 4: The Godfather Strategy & 17-Step Secret Selling System**
   - The 17-step long-form sales page formula (Headline -> Subhead -> Curiosity Bullets -> Agitate Problem -> Unique Solution -> Proof/Credentials -> Benefit Matrix -> Godfather Offer -> Bonuses -> Value Stack -> Price Anchor -> Scarcity -> Ironclad Guarantee -> CTA -> P.S. Warning).
5. **Phase 5: Traffic & The Power 4 Meta Ad Framework**
   - Unit Economics mastery (CPA, CPL, LTV, ROAS).
   - The 4-part ad structure: Hook, Lead, Story/Agitate/Mechanism, Call to Action.
6. **Phase 6: The Magic Lantern Technique & Email Conversion Machine**
   - 3-part video sequence giving 80% value in advance to move leads down the path of goodwill.
   - Plain-text email formatting, slippery slope copy, and send time mastery (Tuesdays at 10 AM, Thursdays at 2 PM, Sundays at 8:30 PM).
   - The 9-word cold lead revival email.
7. **Phase 7: Sales Conversion — "Sell Like a Doctor"**
   - 15-minute Triage Call (qualification) + 45-minute Strategy Session (diagnosis & close).
   - "A prescription without a diagnosis is malpractice."
8. **Phase 8: Automate and Multiply**
   - Scaling winning ad sets, omnichannel expansion, and sales team delegation.
