# Paul Smith's "Sell with a Story" Execution Skill

This skill implements the narrative selling frameworks from Paul Smith's *Sell with a Story*, allowing the agent to structure high-converting, emotionally engaging sales narratives across all marketing channels.

## Core Frameworks & Workflows

1. **The 25 Sales Story Types:**
   - Pre-sale rapport and founder purpose stories.
   - Main pitch stories (Origin, Discovery Journey, Customer Hero, Two Roads).
   - Objection handling stories (Price, Competitor, Timing, Fear of Change).
   - Closing and urgency stories (Cost of Inaction).
2. **The 8-Part Sales Story Spine:**
   - `[1. Hook]` -> `[2. Context (Where/When/Hero/Desire)]` -> `[3. Challenge]` -> `[4. Conflict & Agony]` -> `[5. Climax / Epiphany]` -> `[6. Resolution & After State]` -> `[7. Core Lesson]` -> `[8. Recommended Action (CTA)]`.
3. **The 5 Emotion Techniques:**
   - Tell me, Show me (physical cues), Make me feel (universal experiences), Character familiarity, and Inner/Outer Dialogue.
4. **Data Storytelling:**
   - Framing numbers inside narrative arcs ("How we got here" vs "The discovery journey").
5. **Customer Hero Story Extraction:**
   - The 6-question customer interview framework to turn happy clients into viral case study stories.
