1. **[Alex Hormozi | Grand Slam Offer]** Make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no to you.

2. **[Alex Hormozi | Value Equation]** Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice).

3. **[Alex Hormozi | Pricing Power]** Price is what you pay. Value is what you get. If you increase value by 10x, price becomes irrelevant.

4. **[Alex Hormozi | Commodity Trap]** When you sell the same thing in the same way as everyone else, you are forced to compete on price. That is a race to the bottom.

5. **[Alex Hormozi | The Value Driver]** To charge 10x more, don't work 10x harder. Cut the client's time delay and effort down to near zero.

6. **[Alex Hormozi | Delivery Vehicles]** Always split your offer into DIY (Do It Yourself), DWY (Done With You), and DFY (Done For You). DFY always commands the highest price.

7. **[Alex Hormozi | The Trim & Stack]** List every single obstacle your client faces on their journey, turn each obstacle into a solution, and stack them into your offer.

8. **[Alex Hormozi | Guarantee Power]** The greatest risk in any transaction should always be carried by the seller, not the buyer. If you truly believe in your product, prove it.

9. **[Alex Hormozi | Unconditional Guarantee]** A 100% no-questions-asked money-back guarantee signals ultimate confidence and instantly dissolves buyer skepticism.

10. **[Alex Hormozi | Conditional Guarantee]** 'Do the work, show us your implementation, and if you don't hit the result in 60 days, we pay you double.' High compliance, zero refund abuse.

11. **[Alex Hormozi | Anti-Guarantee]** 'All sales are strictly final because our proprietary assets cannot be unlearned.' Perfect for high-exclusivity and hyper-limited masterminds.

12. **[Alex Hormozi | Bonus Psychology]** A single great bonus can convert someone who was completely on the fence about the core product.

13. **[Alex Hormozi | Bonus Stacking]** Always price your bonuses separately before bundling them. When total bonus value exceeds the ticket price, conversions explode.

14. **[Alex Hormozi | MAGIC Naming]** Name your offer with MAGIC: Magnetic Hook + Avatar Target + Goal Outcome + Interval Timeframe + Container System.

15. **[Alex Hormozi | Urgency vs Scarcity]** Scarcity is fixed supply (only 20 spots). Urgency is fixed time (doors close Friday at midnight). Use both in every campaign.

16. **[Alex Hormozi | Price Elasticity]** Clients who pay the most complain the least, get the best results, and are the most enjoyable to work with.

17. **[Alex Hormozi | Low Price Curse]** When you underprice your service, clients don't value your advice and blame you when they fail to implement.

18. **[Alex Hormozi | Perceived Likelihood]** Case studies and undeniable proof don't just show it works—they increase the perceived likelihood that it will work for *them*.

19. **[Alex Hormozi | Fast Wins]** Give your new customer a massive emotional win within the first 24 hours of buying. It eliminates buyer's remorse forever.

20. **[Alex Hormozi | The Reason Why]** Always give a believable reason for a discount or bonus. If you discount for no reason, prospects assume your product is defective.

21. **[Alex Hormozi | Delivery Cube]** Package your offer across 5 delivery media: 1-on-1, Small Group, One-to-Many, Digital/Software, and Physical Artifacts.

22. **[Alex Hormozi | The Effort Multiplier]** People will pay 100x more for a liposuction pill than a gym membership because the perceived effort is zero.

23. **[Alex Hormozi | Speed Premium]** Whoever delivers the dream outcome the fastest in your industry wins all the high-margin market share.

24. **[Alex Hormozi | Guaranteed Outcome]** If you cannot guarantee the outcome, guarantee the effort: 'We work with you for free until you reach the goal.'

25. **[Alex Hormozi | Social Proof Anchors]** Never say 'many clients love this'. Say: '416 verified founders across 22 niches achieved an average 3.4x ROI in 45 days.'

26. **[Alex Hormozi | The Risk Inversion]** When you eliminate risk for the buyer, price resistance vanishes.

27. **[Alex Hormozi | Niche Selection]** Pick a market with 4 traits: Massive Pain, Purchasing Power, Easy to Target, and Growing Market Size.

28. **[Alex Hormozi | Starving Crowd]** The best hot dog stand in the world will fail in a cemetery. Put your stand in front of a starving crowd coming out of a club at 2:00 AM.

29. **[Alex Hormozi | Offer Uniqueness]** If they can compare you directly to your competitor, your offer isn't differentiated enough. Invent your own category.

30. **[Alex Hormozi | Price Anchoring]** Anchor against the cost of the problem, not the cost of your time. If a bad hire costs $50,000, a $5,000 recruiting system is a 90% discount.

31. **[Alex Hormozi | The 1-to-10 Rule]** Your total bundle perceived value should be at least 10 times the asking price.

32. **[Alex Hormozi | Sweetener Bonuses]** Never drop your price to close a hesitant lead. Instead, throw in an extra high-value sweetener bonus that costs you nothing to deliver.

33. **[Alex Hormozi | Productization]** Turn your custom bespoke service into a repeatable 5-step branded proprietary protocol.

34. **[Alex Hormozi | The Naming Test]** If your product name doesn't immediately tell the buyer who it is for and what result they get, change the name.

35. **[Alex Hormozi | Guaranteed Results]** A bold guarantee forces your company to build a superior fulfillment process.

36. **[Alex Hormozi | Charge More]** Raising your prices allows you to spend more money on customer acquisition and deliver a world-class customer experience.

38. **[Alex Hormozi | Bonus Naming]** Name your bonuses after the specific objection they destroy. Example: 'The 15-Minute Fast Implementation Cheatsheet' destroys the 'I have no time' objection.

40. **[Alex Hormozi | The Walk-Away Power]** The person who needs the deal the least always commands the most respect and highest price at the negotiating table.
