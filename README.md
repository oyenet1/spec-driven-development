## 💬 Simple Prompts You Can Tell Your AI

You don't need fancy prompt engineering. Just talk normally:

### 1. Make Your Story Better
> *"Here is a rough story of what happened to me: [Paste your story]. Use the storytelling skill to turn this into an emotional sales story that makes people want to buy my product."*

### 2. Write an Ad for Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok
> *"Write a simple, high-converting Facebook Ad for my product [Product Name]. Use the Power 4 Ad formula (Hook, Lead, Story, Call to Action) so people stop scrolling and click."*

### 3. Write a Long Sales Page
> *"Use Sabri Suby's 17-step formula to write a complete sales page for my [Service/Product]. Make sure it agitates the pain, shows proof, gives bonuses, and has a 100% money-back guarantee."*

### 4. Write a 60-Second Video Ad Script
> *"Give me a 60-second video script for TikTok/Reels. Tell me what to say into the camera, what words to put on screen, and what B-roll video to show."*

### 5. Write an Email Sequence
> *"Write 4 simple plain-text emails to sell my product. Tell me which days to send them and what time to hit send."*

### 6. Sell on WhatsApp in Nigeria
> *"I sell shoes in Lagos. Give me a 4-part WhatsApp status plan for today and a simple chat script to close customers who ask 'How much?'"*

### 7. Write Full Marketing Copy in Another Language (Spanish, French, Pidgin, etc.)
> *"Write a complete 17-step sales page in **Spanish** for my fitness coaching program. Use Sabri Suby's Godfather Offer formula and Hormozi's value stack."*

> *"Write a 3-part Facebook ad campaign in **Nigerian Pidgin** selling solar generators in Lagos. Focus on eliminating the fear of fake products with 100% Free Nationwide Delivery."*

### 8. Write a Multi-Language SEO Blog with Hreflang Tags & Localized Schema
> *"Use the `blog-multilingual` skill to write a high-value blog post on 'How to Get B2B Clients' in English, Spanish, and French. Include hreflang tags, localized JSON-LD schema, and adapt the examples for each country."*

### 9. AI Video Creator & 10-Second Scene Director (Google Veo, Sora, Kling, Runway)
> *"I have a product called **[Product Name]** for **[Target Audience]** (Duration: 60s, Extra Details: [Offer/Pricing]). Break this down into six continuous 10-second scenes with zero context loss. Include professional camera movements, lighting, exact text-to-video AI visual prompts, timed 20-word voiceovers, on-screen text, sound design, and scene transitions."*

## 🧠 5 Simple Marketing Truths to Never Forget

1. **Don't only shout at the 3% who want to buy today:** 97% of people have the problem but aren't ready to buy yet. Educate them with free helpful guides, and they will buy from you first.
2. **Sell like a doctor, not a pushy salesperson:** A good doctor asks where it hurts before writing a prescription. Always ask what's wrong before you pitch your product.
3. **Make results fast and easy:** If you make the result take less time and zero hard work, you can charge 10 times more.
4. **Facts tell, stories sell:** People make buying choices with their gut and feelings, and then use logic to explain it. Tell a simple story of someone who struggled and won.
5. **In Nigeria, proof kills fear:** People are afraid of losing their money to fake sellers. Show real videos of your shop, show delivery receipts, show happy customer chats, and offer free delivery.
